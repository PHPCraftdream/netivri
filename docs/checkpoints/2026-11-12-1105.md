## Decisions
- Silent hops are caught by a negotiation budget up to the target request (connect, TLS, SOCKS5 negotiation) instead of a whole-attempt deadline, so slow-but-working CONNECT replies are not cut and a failure after the target was sent is never blamed.
- Dead hops behind lazy carriers are quarantined by a soft streak (5) in the pool library rather than by blaming carrier errors on the hop (carrier errors keep counting against the node only).
- Non-ASCII hop hosts are rejected at config validation (stdlib has no IDNA); `_` is allowed everywhere.
- An HTTP hop behind a lazy node that stays silent after CONNECT still gets no blame: CONNECT is its first message, retry is unsafe.
