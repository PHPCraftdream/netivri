## Decisions
- Link forms: each protocol's link must be importable by the REAL Mihomo importer and by netivri's subscription parser (unit-tested through `convert.ConvertsV2Ray`); where the importer reads no pin (TUIC, Snell, Sudoku, ShadowQUIC, TrustTunnel) the Mihomo YAML snippet is authoritative and `Link` (uri) returns a clear error.
- Reality camouflage dial: DIRECT and outside the client filter (verified in CI; a leak through the user's VPN node otherwise).
- e2e standard: angel A with the server, angel B imports the generated link through a loopback subscription, echo through A's account route with node recorders; advertise `::ffff:127.0.0.1`; after any step that restarts A's core or makes A drop B's probes restart angel B (dead nodes are re-probed with 45 s base plus backoff).
- `account set-clients` currently restarts the whole core (`listenerFingerprint`); making it in place is task #172 (agent running).
- Coverage floors are never lowered; `internal/servers/servertest` has its own documented floor line (25).
- One port for several protocols: TCP/UDP already may share a number; same-transport sharing needs a front mux in the broker (design in docs/design/servers.md); ambiguous combinations are refused at `server add`.
- Reviewer "fail" verdicts of rush runs are mostly "no test executed": findings are verified by me against the source before acting.
