# Field measurement, 2026-10-20

One Windows 10 machine, one account bound to a provider and the exit country
US, which holds a single node: VLESS over TCP with Reality and
`xtls-rprx-vision`. Measured with `netivri diag dial --account <login>` (5
samples, default idle ladder) from the build of `4d54eb3`. Node names and
addresses are not recorded.

## Phases of a fresh connection (milliseconds)

| Phase | p50 | p90 | max |
|---|---|---|---|
| dns (system resolver, node host) | 0.6 | 54.1 | 54.1 |
| tcp (connect to the node) | 14.6 | 39.4 | 39.4 |
| tls (Reality handshake) | 18.8 | 27.6 | 27.6 |
| first byte (VLESS request, exit connect to the probe host, response) | 144.2 | 150.8 | 150.8 |

Failures: none in the 5 samples (dns, tcp, tls, first byte) and none in the
5 idle attempts.

Idle survival of a prepared transport (TCP + Reality done, no VLESS request
sent): every wait of 0.5, 1, 2, 4 and 8 s was followed by a successful
request. Suggested pool TTL: 4 s (half of the longest wait).

## Reading

- A fresh connection reaches its first byte in about 178 ms at the median:
  0.3 % dns, 8 % tcp, 11 % Reality and 81 % the way through the chain to the
  target and back, which no pool or hedge can shorten.
- A pre-handshaked transport pool would save tcp + tls, about 33 ms per new
  connection (19 %). The gate in [latency.md](../design/latency.md)
  (Reality phase of at least about 30 % of the time to first byte) is not met,
  although idle survival (at least 1 s) is: the pool is not built on this
  evidence.
- The node host resolves in under a millisecond after the first lookup and
  has a single address, so a DNS cache and `tcp-concurrent` have nothing to
  gain here.
- The earlier warm-up probe of this node (619 ms, 11 of 187 probes failed at
  the 5 s timeout, from the build installed at that time) is not reproduced
  by 5 + 5 requests; the probe measured a whole request under whatever load
  the machine had, and a failure rate of about 6 % needs more samples than
  this run. The hedge counters (`status.affinity.hedges`, `hedge_wins`) and
  `netivri stats` collect that over time.
